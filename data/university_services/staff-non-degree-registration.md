# Non-Degree Staff Registration

This source describes the process for Carnegie Mellon staff members who want to take coursework without seeking a degree.

## Petition and registration process

Staff members must read the Faculty/Staff Non-Degree Guide and submit a Faculty/Staff Non-Degree Petition by the first day of classes for each semester of enrollment. Current staff do not need to submit an official transcript. After University Registrar approval, they may use Student Information Online (SIO) to plan and register for eligible courses after Registration Week ends.

## Staff tuition benefits

Staff members should confirm eligibility through the Human Resources Tuition Benefits for Staff webpage and obtain supervisor permission before applying for tuition benefits. Applications should be submitted after the course schedule is finalized and registration is complete, and by the first day of classes.

## Important deadlines

Staff non-degree students follow the Academic Calendar. To receive a tuition adjustment, they must drop a course before the add/drop deadline. They do not receive Drop Vouchers.
