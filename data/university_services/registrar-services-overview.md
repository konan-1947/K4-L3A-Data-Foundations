# University Registrar Services Overview

Our team provides services to students, alumni, faculty, and departments related to student verifications, course scheduling and registration, learning spaces, final exams and grading, academic records, diplomas, academic offerings, VA benefits certification, and more. 

As a unit within Enrollment Management, we aspire to be an institutional keystone of student data stewardship, learning spaces, and campus-serving technology. We ensure student academic record integrity and support academic programs, while informing and facilitating academic policy compliance. Our team members, along with our partners in The HUB, look forward to providing high quality services.
